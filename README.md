<h1 align="center">Game's Up</h1>

<p align="center">
  See which friends play the same games, and get a game going with them through Discord.
</p>

Game's Up is for groups of friends who play together and organise it on Discord. Instead of asking around in a channel to find out who owns what and who is free, you keep a library of the games you play, see where it overlaps with your friends, and send a call to play: the people who share the game get a Discord message they can answer with one tap, and everyone sees who is in.

## Features

- **Shared libraries:** add the games you play, by search or by importing from Steam, and see which friends own and want to play the same ones.
- **Calls to play:** pick one to five games, a time (now or later) and an audience, and the Discord bot invites exactly the friends who can join.
- **One-tap answers in Discord:** recipients answer "I'm interested", "Can't make it" or "I'll be late" from the message itself, and one running updates message per call keeps everyone current without a flood of pings.
- **Friends-first discovery:** profiles are visible to friends by default; you choose which shared Discord servers widen your discovery audience.
- **Discord server hub:** server admins can post a Game's Up hub where members join, start a call to play, and manage their games and friends without leaving Discord.
- **Temporary voice and text space:** a call can open a private text and voice channel pair in the Game's Up Discord server, deleted automatically after the session.
- **Installable web app:** works in any modern browser and installs as an app on desktop and phone.
- **No tracking:** Game's Up collects no telemetry or crash reports.

## Get it

Game's Up is a web app: sign in with Discord in a current desktop or mobile browser, and install it from the browser if you want it on your home screen or desktop.
